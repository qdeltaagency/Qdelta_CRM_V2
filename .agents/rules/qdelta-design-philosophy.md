# Qdelta Design Philosophy & Operational Core Rules

## 1. Quality of Experience Over Number of Screens
- **Do NOT optimize for the quantity of screens.**
- **Optimize relentlessly for the quality of the experience.**
- Never ship superficial placeholders, generic admin templates, or crowded clutter.
- Every workflow must solve a real operator need with clarity and speed.

## 2. Emotional Tone & UX Principles
Every screen and interaction in Qdelta must feel:
1. **Intentional**: Every button, metric, badge, and interaction has a clear, unambiguous purpose.
2. **Calm**: Generous breathing room, subtle 1px border hierarchy, soft natural shadows, balanced white space—zero visual chaos.
3. **Fast**: Instant response, keyboard-friendly actions (`⌘K`), 1-click clipboard feedback, fluid micro-transitions.
4. **Premium**: High-end typography, refined color tokens, cohesive icons, bespoke styling tailored for a top-tier digital studio.
5. **Easy to Understand**: An operator must grasp business health, cash flow, sprint velocity, and next actions in under 10 seconds.

## 3. Original Visual Identity
- **Do not use generic CRM templates.**
- **Do not clone HubSpot, Salesforce, Linear, or generic reference templates.**
- Take inspiration from modern design patterns while maintaining an original, distinct **Qdelta visual language**.
